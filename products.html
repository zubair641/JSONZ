Non-Return Valve (NRV)

Non-return valve is a single-way valve that allows the fluid to flow only in one direction. Main importance of non-return valves is their working of allowing flow in the downstream direction and preventing the flow in the upstream direction.

Non-Return Valves are also known as NRVs. It is usually small, simple. There are various types of non-return valves but the main function is the same. It is also known as one-way valve, check valve, clack valve, reflux valve, or retention valve. It is available in different sizes.

Function: Allows flow one way → Blocks reverse flow
